Analysis tools and Datasets

LandsatTS

An R package that enables users to extract nearly four decades of Landsat satellite surface reflectance measurements for sample locations anywhere on Earth and generate high-quality time series of vegetation greenness for ecosystem monitoring, by Berner et al.
Accompanying paper: LandsatTS: an R package to facilitate retrieval, cleaning, cross-calibration, and phenological modeling of Landsat time series data  (Berner et al. 2023, Ecography)

TuktuTools

An R package for studying caribou movement ecology and spatial patterns, by Gurarie et al.
User guide: Estimating caribou calving dates and locations using the TuktuTools Package (Couriot and Gurarie 2022)

Permeability

An R package for animal movement data with a set of functions to quantify barrier (i.e., road) permeability, fit and compare models, and plot and map predictions, by Barbour et al.
Under peer review (July 2025)

Arctic Plant Aboveground Biomass Synthesis Dataset, Pan-Arctic, 1998 - 2022

A dataset synthesizing and harmonizing 32 datasets on biomass of lichen, bryophytes, herbs, shrubs, and trees across the Arctic.
 Accompanying paper: The Arctic Abovegound Biomass Sythesis Dataset: A paper detailing the methods and results of the APABSD (Berner et al. 2024, Scientific Data)

Gridded 30-meter resolution estimates of aboveground plant biomass, woody plant biomass, and woody plant dominance across the Arctic tundra biome (2020)

Maps of aboveground plant and woody plant biomass across the entire Pan Arctic for 2020.
Accompanying paper: Next generation vegetation maps: Aboveground plant biomass and woody dominance mapped at 30m resolution across the tundra biome (Orndahl et al. 2025, Remote Sensing of Environment)

Fortymile caribou herdrange isopleths, occurrence distributions, and associated vegetation changedata, Alaska and Yukon Territory (1991 - 2021)

An extensive dataset that is publicly available on the Arctic Data Center. The dataset includes population size estimates for the herd from 1920 to 2022; range isopleths (georeferenced outlines) for every year from 1992-2020 and seasons within each year; heat maps of animal spatial density, which highlights heavily-used habitat areas; and changes in vegetation communities over the same time period, correlated with caribou density and land-cover types.

Caribou Sound Library

This library consists of sounds made by caribou and captive reindeer as part of a study from the Gurarie Wildlife Ecology lab. We use acoustic recorders on the land and on the animals to collect these sounds, along with soundscapes from the surrounding environment.

rf R to GEE

An R package with tools to convert random forest models generated in R to data formats accepted by Google Earth Engine (GEE), including scripts and sample data to take random forest models fitted via the ranger or randomForest package in R and convert them into the 'tree' based string format required for import into GEE.
